Version 1.0.22 - Fixed a backup issue where your card images could pile up as duplicate copies in the backup folder after reinstalling or restoring. Backups are smarter now: unchanged images are left exactly as they are instead of being rewritten every time, so synced folders (like Nextcloud or Syncthing) stop lighting up with constant changes. Any duplicate copies you already have get cleaned up automatically on your next backup, making backups smaller and a little faster too.
