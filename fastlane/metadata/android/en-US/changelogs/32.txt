Version 1.0.31 - New option in Settings: a soft glow at the screen edge announces the card a moment before it appears, on the side where your card lives - no more cards materializing under your thumb mid-tap. You choose how long it glows (1-5 s) and how noticeable it is. If a card arrives while you are typing, the glow plays right after the keyboard closes. Also hardened the overlay window lifecycle against rare stuck or orphaned cards
